### Size ceiling (SC-006)

A raw `obsidian vault=TestVault-Obsidian-CLI-MCP tags format=json counts` call over the same 2,015 tags returned **112,736 bytes**, which is **55.95 bytes per entry**. The average tag was 14.95 bytes including its `#`, and every count was one digit. That leaves a fixed overhead of 40 bytes per entry.

Derived ceiling: 10,485,760 ÷ 55.95 ≈ **187,000** unique tags for names of about 14 bytes, or about 140,000–200,000 for names of 10–30 bytes. `docs/tools/tags.md` now states the measured figure, labelled as derived from this measurement, in place of the plan-stage estimate. The figure falls inside the R11 estimate (150,000–250,000), towards its lower end.
